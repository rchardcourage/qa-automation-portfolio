How I work

I do not report how many tests ran. I report whether it is safe to ship.

Quality is a trust problem before it is a testing problem. I plan coverage around the places where trust breaks fastest, say what was tested and what was not, and put clear go or no-go criteria in place before the time pressure arrives.

Risk first

Payments, identity and money held in escrow get tested before anything else. Effort follows risk, not the order of the backlog.

Coverage you can explain

Leadership hears what was covered, what was left out and why. A coverage plan over a feature list, not a pile of green ticks.

Release decisions, not hopes

Go or no-go criteria agreed up front. I own the call and the evidence behind it.

Example release gate
PASSMoney-flow scenarios: wallet, payment, refund
PASSRegression on core journeys, API and UI
PASSNo open high-priority defects on trust-critical flows
HOLDAny unexplained gap in coverage on a money flow
